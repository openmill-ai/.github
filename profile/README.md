# OpenMill

**The open-source operating system for GPU clusters.**

OpenMill turns GPU capacity into a complete AI cloud. It serves open models through an
OpenAI-compatible API, trains them, and meters and bills their use, all on your own hardware and
under your own brand.

## What's coming

- **OpenMill Serve** serves models. It provides an OpenAI-compatible API, autoscaling and
  scale-to-zero, and installs with one command per cluster.
- **OpenMill Train** trains models. It provides a training API for your own fine-tuning and
  reinforcement-learning loops, plus managed fine-tuning and distillation jobs.

## Who it's for

- **Data center and GPU cloud operators** who want to sell AI services, not just GPU-hours.
- **AI inference providers** who want to run a token business without building the serving stack.
- **Enterprise AI teams** who want their models, data and agents to stay inside their own
  boundary.

## Open source

The core will be released under the Apache-2.0 licence. OpenMill is in active development, and the
first public release is coming soon. Watch this organization to hear about it first.
